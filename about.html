About us

Community rooted.
Environment focused.

Green World Advocates is based in Kisumu, Kenya, with a public mission focused on biodiversity and food security within vulnerable communities.

Our purpose

A practical bridge between people and planet.

Environmental challenges are experienced locally: through food insecurity, depleted landscapes, waste, reduced opportunity and pressure on household livelihoods. Green World Advocates exists to help communities respond through practical, participatory action.

The organisation combines conservation, agribusiness, environmental awareness and youth/community engagement to build resilience in ways people can see and take ownership of.

Mission

Support communities to protect nature while strengthening everyday resilience.

We connect biodiversity protection and food security with community participation, practical skills and locally relevant action.

Vision

Communities where healthy ecosystems and secure livelihoods reinforce each other.

A future in which environmental stewardship is part of how communities create opportunity, wellbeing and long-term resilience.

Our values

How we choose to work.

Community ownership

Solutions are stronger when people closest to the challenge shape the response.

Practical sustainability

We favour actions that communities can understand, maintain and build on.

Dignity & inclusion

Environmental progress should widen participation and strengthen human dignity.

Learning

We adapt through field experience, community feedback and partnership.

Partnership

Durable change requires cooperation across communities, institutions and supporters.

Future generations

We work with long-term ecological and social wellbeing in mind.

Where we work

Grounded in Kisumu. Connected to wider community needs.

Green World Advocates’ Facebook profile identifies the organisation in Kisumu, Nyanza Province, Kenya. The website therefore keeps Kisumu at the centre of its public identity while presenting the work in a way that can grow with future programmes and partnerships.
