Biodiversity

Restore nature where communities live.

Biodiversity work focuses on the landscapes, trees, habitats and local stewardship practices that underpin resilient communities.

Programme focus

Restoration is more than planting trees.

Meaningful restoration connects planting with care, survival, local knowledge and community ownership. Green World Advocates frames biodiversity as a practical community asset that supports climate resilience, food systems and wellbeing.

  • Community tree planting and stewardship
  • Environmental awareness and local conservation
  • Protection of ecosystem services
  • Youth participation in restoration activities
  • Partnerships that support long-term landscape recovery
