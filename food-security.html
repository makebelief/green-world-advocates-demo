Food security

Resilient food systems start with resilient communities.

Food security work connects agriculture, climate awareness, livelihoods and responsible use of natural resources.

Programme focus

Agriculture that supports both livelihoods and landscapes.

Green World Advocates’ public profile highlights food security as a core concern. The programme direction centres on practical agribusiness awareness, sustainable production and community resilience.

  • Climate-smart agriculture awareness
  • Small-scale agribusiness and livelihood development
  • Soil, water and resource stewardship
  • Community learning around resilient food systems
  • Linking environmental care to household wellbeing
Our lens

Food security is ecological security.

Healthy land

Productive farms depend on functioning soils, water, biodiversity and climate resilience.

Viable livelihoods

Sustainability lasts when families can connect environmental practices with real economic value.

Supporting biodiversity, food security and community-led environmental action from Kisumu, Kenya.
