Climate action

Make environmental responsibility visible.

Community climate action becomes meaningful when people can participate directly — through clean-ups, awareness, restoration and better everyday environmental practices.

Programme focus

From awareness to hands-on action.

Environmental awareness matters most when it changes how communities engage with public spaces, waste, natural resources and climate risks.

  • Community clean-up activities
  • Waste awareness and responsible disposal
  • Climate and environmental education
  • Public participation and local mobilisation
  • Practical action linked to broader conservation goals
Community action cycle
