Impact

We measure progress by what communities can sustain.

Impact is not only the number of activities delivered. It is the strength of local participation, environmental improvement and the ability of communities to continue building on the work.

What progress looks like

A practical impact framework.

Ecological improvement

Healthier landscapes, restored vegetation and more responsible use of natural resources.

Food resilience

Communities with stronger knowledge and options around sustainable food production and livelihoods.

Youth participation

Young people engaged through sport, skills, awareness and local environmental activity.

Community ownership

More local participation in designing, delivering and sustaining action.

Environmental responsibility

Better awareness and visible participation in clean-ups, waste responsibility and conservation.

Partnership capacity

Relationships that expand knowledge, resources and opportunities for community-led work.

Accountability

Clear activities. Clear learning. Clear next steps.
